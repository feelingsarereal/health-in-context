## What it is

The WHO conceptual framework separates two kinds of determinant [2]. **Structural determinants** are the socioeconomic and political context, the mechanisms that stratify a society, and the socioeconomic position people end up in — by income, education, occupation, social class, gender and race or ethnicity. **Intermediary determinants** are the routes through which that position reaches health: material circumstances such as housing, neighbourhood and the means to buy food; psychosocial circumstances such as stressors, relationships and social support; behavioural and biological factors; and the health system itself [2].

Socioeconomic status has been described as underlying health care, environmental exposure and health behaviour at once, with chronic stress as a further pathway [15].

## How this app uses it

<!-- framing -->
The association menu orders its suggestions by these tiers: finances, employment, education, the social and cultural environment, social insurance and the justice system first; then housing and environment, nutrition and water; then relationships, loss, family, trauma and upbringing; then health behaviours. The tier assignment of each ICD-11 block is the builder's reading of the framework, and the menu says so.
