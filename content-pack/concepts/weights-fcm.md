## What it is

<!-- framing -->
This app borrows the idea of signed, weighted causal links from fuzzy cognitive mapping. A fuzzy cognitive map joins causal concepts with links of graded strength and propagates effects through them [58]; in its participatory form, people draw the links themselves and give each a strength between −1 and +1 [59].

## How this app uses it

<!-- framing -->
A weight's sign must match the link's polarity, and its size is a judgement, not a measured effect size. Weights feed three heuristics: a loop's strength (the product of its weights), the weight of a path between two circumstances, and the optional what-if pulse, which shows only the direction in which a change might ripple. None of these is a prediction, and the app labels each one.
